# 14. TRANSFERS

Implement atomic transfers between wallets.

Each transfer must include:

- Unique transfer ID.
- Source wallet.
- Destination wallet.
- Amount.
- Date.
- Optional comment.

A valid transfer creates two linked ledger entries:

- Debit the source wallet.
- Credit the destination wallet.

Reject transfers when:

- The source and destination are the same.
- The amount is zero or negative.
- The amount exceeds the available source balance without an authorized overdraft.
- Any validation fails.

Both ledger entries must be created or reversed in a single database transaction.

Transfers must not count as consolidated income or expenses.

The sum of all wallet balances must remain unchanged after an internal transfer.

Prevent duplicate transfers when requests are retried.

# 15. CASH WITHDRAWALS

Support two distinct withdrawal types.

### Type A: Withdrawal to external, untracked cash

Debit the source bank wallet and record a withdrawal to an untracked destination.

This is not automatically an expense.

### Type B: Withdrawal to a tracked cash wallet

Debit the source bank wallet and credit the destination cash wallet.

The combined wallet balance remains unchanged.

Both operations must be traceable in the wallet history.

Never count a withdrawal as both a transfer and an expense.
